If eligible, you can

  • Live in Canada as a permanent resident
  • Work, study and settle in any province or territory
  • Access public healthcare once provincial rules are met
  • Include eligible family members in your plan
  • Progress toward Canadian citizenship

You will need

  • Skilled work experience in an eligible TEER occupation
  • Enough points for the Federal Skilled Worker pass mark, or another Express Entry route
  • Language, education and admissibility documents
