About the skilled program

Points-based and ranked

Canadian skilled migration is points-tested. Your assessment gives an indicative Federal Skilled Worker result and an Express Entry CRS estimate from your answers. If you don't meet the minimums yet, you may need to build your profile first. If your score is competitive, it's worth exploring your options further.

Any real application needs evidence for every claim you make, so keep your documents ready.

How long you can stay

Permanent residence has no fixed end date, as long as you meet residency obligations and follow Canadian law.

Including family

Eligible family members, such as a spouse or partner and dependent children, may be included in your plan.
